How your menu is controlled (important)

Your menu hierarchy is driven ONLY by categories, not products.

categories.type = 'main' → Top-level menu sections

categories.type = 'sub' → Clickable POS menu groups

products.category_id → ALWAYS points to a sub category

So yes — main & sub categories fully control the menu flow.
